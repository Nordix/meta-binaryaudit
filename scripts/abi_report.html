Note on ABI CHANGED and binary compatibility: When a library's SONAME is unchanged (e.g. libcurl.so.4 remains libcurl.so.4), the upstream maintainers are guaranteeing that existing compiled binaries will continue to work. The ABI CHANGED tag reflects a formal type-level change detected by abidiff, such as a renamed enum value, a struct size increase due to new fields, or an internal sentinel constant changing. These do not affect a well-behaved consumer of the library. The real signal to watch is SYMBOLS REMOVED: symbols that were removed will break any binary that referenced them.
